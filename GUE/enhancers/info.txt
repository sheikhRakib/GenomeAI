Enhancer dataset: source and train/dev/test split

Source
------
Binary enhancer benchmark attributed to Geng et al. (2022), distributed as the
"enhancers" task in InstaDeepAI/nucleotide_transformer_downstream_tasks.
Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpc.2022.106822
Dataset card:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/InstaDeepAI/nucleotide_transformer_downstream_tasks/blob/96d86d567d4cd33536e49b429dc7983121619a08/README.md

The supplied data contains 14,968 training rows and 400 test rows, with two
columns: sequence,label. No validation split was supplied.

Split method
------------
Read the 14,968 original training rows from `raw_train.csv`. Split individual
rows using sklearn.model_selection.train_test_split, stratifying only by label:

    train_rows, dev_rows = train_test_split(
        rows,
        test_size=0.1,
        random_state=42,
        stratify=[row["label"] for row in rows],
    )
